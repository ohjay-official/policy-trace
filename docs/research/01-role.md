## 2. The official posting does not say "entry level"

The official title and body on Ashby and supabase.com/careers are **"Developer Relations Engineer"**. The official text does **not** use the words "entry level", "junior", "senior", or a years-of-experience requirement.

Some aggregators attached seniority labels to this family of postings. Those labels are **not** on the official posting:

- Built In Singapore labeled a Singapore-remote variant "Entry level", and later reported the listing was removed at 04:48 SGT on 22 Sep 2026: [https://builtinsingapore.com/job/developer-relations-engineer/10787973](https://builtinsingapore.com/job/developer-relations-engineer/10787973)
- Built In labeled the London variant "Entry level": [https://builtin.com/job/developer-relations-engineer-london-uk/10787523](https://builtin.com/job/developer-relations-engineer-london-uk/10787523)
- Letsworkremotely labeled the New York variant "Junior", "3–5 Years", and "Bachelors Degree", with a posted date of 3 Nov 2026 and an expiration of 19 Nov 2026: [https://www.letsworkremotely.com/job/developer-relations-engineer-new-york-ny/](https://www.letsworkremotely.com/job/developer-relations-engineer-new-york-ny/). Those experience and degree figures do **not** appear in the official Ashby text for the NY or Global postings.

**Inference:** Treating the Global posting as officially "entry level" would overclaim. The official bar is skill- and evidence-based (engineering + content + public representation), not a stated junior title. Separately, Ant Wilson wrote in 2023 that Supabase "very rarely hire[s] juniors" while still hiring skilled younger people (see [02-people.md](02-people.md)). How that 2023 culture post maps onto this 2026 Marketing DevRel posting is not stated.

## 3. Location and remote rules

**Stated in this Global posting:**

> Fully Remote. We hire globally. We believe you can do your best work from anywhere. There are no Supabase offices, but we provide a WeWork membership or co-working allowance you can use anywhere in the world.

> We operate asynchronously and trust you to manage your own time. You know what needs to be done and when.

The careers page repeats the same remote, async, and co-working points: [https://supabase.com/careers](https://supabase.com/careers).

## 4. Quoted responsibilities ("What You'll Do")

From the official Global posting:

- **Make content** — "Publish compelling technical content, especially video, to help developers learn Supabase quickly"
- **Build demos and tutorials** — "Ship real-world apps using Supabase and tools like Next.js, React, and Stripe. Write guides that others can follow and remix"
- **Represent Supabase** — "Speak at meetups, livestream builds, and engage with the developer ecosystem. You'll be a visible and trusted voice of the platform"
- **Support and grow the community** — "Celebrate contributors, answer questions, highlight cool projects, and bring developer feedback to the team"
- **Collaborate across the company** — "Work with engineering, product, and growth to amplify launches, prioritize content, and reduce friction for new users"

## 5. Quoted "You Might Be a Good Fit If"

- "You've built full-stack apps and enjoy sharing your knowledge"
- "You create content that makes people want to open their code editor"
- "You're equally comfortable writing a video script and TypeScript"
- "You're energized by community whether it's on Discord, Twitter, YouTube, or in person"
- "You're self-directed, organized, and excited to contribute to open source"

## 6. Quoted requirements

- "Strong engineering skills, especially with JavaScript or TypeScript, Postgres, and modern frontend frameworks (React, Svelte, Vue, etc.)"
- "Experience creating technical content (written or video)"
- "Comfortable representing a product publicly (on camera, on stage, or online)"

## 7. Quoted bonus points

- "A growing audience on YouTube, Twitch, or Twitter"
- "Prior experience in DevRel or open source"
- "Have used (and loved) Supabase"

Audience, prior DevRel, and prior Supabase use are **bonus**, not listed as hard requirements.

## 8. Role framing and "Why This Role Matters"

**Stated:**

> We're looking for a Developer Relations Engineer to join our team and help more developers discover, learn, and build with Supabase. You'll create high-impact content, build real-world projects, and represent Supabase across communities and events. If you're equally energized by writing code and teaching others, this is the role for you.

> Supabase is growing fast with 350,000+ developers, 1,000+ OSS contributors, and a thriving open source ecosystem. Our users are builders, startup founders, weekend hackers, and engineers scaling to millions of users. DevRel is how we meet them where they are: through content, community, and code.

> We're building a community of communities that brings together developers from many backgrounds, including first-time open source contributors.

The "About the Team" block on the same posting also states "~400 team members", "60+ countries", "20+ languages spoken", "Over $1B raised (including our $500M Series F)", and "540,000+ community members". The careers page uses "350+" team members and the same $1B / 540,000+ figures: [https://supabase.com/careers](https://supabase.com/careers). These numbers are quoted as the company states them; they were not independently audited here.

**Inference:** The official description is a builder-teacher role (code + content + community), housed in Marketing, with video called out as especially important and remixable shipped demos as a first-class deliverable.
