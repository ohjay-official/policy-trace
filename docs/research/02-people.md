## D. Leadership on hiring and culture (first-party)

### Ant Wilson (CTO, co-founder) — "Who We Hire at Supabase"

Source: [https://supabase.com/blog/who-we-hire](https://supabase.com/blog/who-we-hire). JSON-LD author Ant Wilson (`github.com/awalias`), published **9 December 2022**.

Stated traits:

- **Extreme effectiveness when working asynchronously.** "People who require a lot of face-to-face collaboration are unlikely to be successful at Supabase. Major projects have gone from inception, through implementation, to marketing and launch without a single synchronous meeting." "Meetings are used as a last resort."
- **Egoless.** "Supa-unblockers" take work off others' plates. At the time of writing: "We don’t have managers," everyone does IC work, self-manages, and has access to metrics and product principles.
- **Kaizen mindset.** Small, frequent improvements; new starters "can start contributing in their first week."
- **Default to action.** Preferred language is "why not now?" rather than "Let’s meet next week to discuss."
- **Unreasonable levels of ambition.** "The right people don’t need to run a customer survey to know what to work on next, they’re obsessed with solving their own problems."
- **A founder mindset.** "We have a bias towards people who previously ran their own companies." "It’s not the credentials that we value most, but the mindset. We expect everyone who works at Supabase to act like a business owner."

The same post describes docs as part of the product: feature teams write the docs; the docs team consults on structure. Inter-team work should not be "throwing tasks over the fence."

**Caution:** The post is from December 2022 (team then described as "60 people spread across 25 different countries"). Org size, management structure, and DevRel reporting may have changed. The current careers page still lists the same five traits (Egoless, Kaizen Mindset, Truth Seeking, Batteries Included, Undeniable): [https://supabase.com/careers](https://supabase.com/careers).

### Ant Wilson — "Why we'll stay remote"

Source: [https://supabase.com/blog/why-supabase-remote](https://supabase.com/blog/why-supabase-remote). JSON-LD author Ant Wilson, published **5 August 2023**.

Directly relevant quotes:

> There’s a large portion of the population who [require a lot of face time to get their job done], and without casting any negative judgement, we have to be open and honest with candidates that if they’re in this camp - they’ll likely be unhappy working at Supabase.

> At Supabase, we very rarely hire juniors. That’s not to say we don’t hire young people, since there are plenty of highly skilled young people who can out-ship people twice their age. But in terms of team make-up, our team is very highly skewed towards seniors and leads.

> When interviewing candidates a common question we get is around working hours, and the honest answer is that we don’t care when you work.

> Most of the people we hire have a background in open source and contribute to large projects like PostgreSQL where most things run asynchronously.

> Hire people who are: Smart. Get Things Done. Everything else is a Nice to Have. Including proximity.

On pay, he wrote that Supabase does not do geo-adjusted pay, and that outside major US cities pay is "easily… in the 95th percentile (in many cases in the 99th percentile)." That is a 2023 claim; current comp bands were **not** verified.

**Inference:** "Very rarely hire juniors" is a 2023 culture statement about team makeup, not a sentence in the 2026 DevRel posting. The posting itself has no seniority or years-of-experience requirement. The same essay explicitly leaves the door open for younger people who can ship at a high level.

### Paul Copplestone (CEO, co-founder)

First-party and on-the-record comments:

- In a Felicis interview, "Supabase CEO Paul Copplestone on Building for Builders": [https://www.felicis.com/blog/paul-copplestone-supabase](https://www.felicis.com/blog/paul-copplestone-supabase). He describes a flat, open, distributed culture, wanting people with "a developer mindset," curiosity, hard work, and "intellectual honesty." Former founders "fit into this distributed culture where we can give them a lot of autonomy and leave them to it." "We don't really tell people what to do. They tell us what they're doing based on the feedback that they get from the community and from our priority customers." Launch weeks are shared shipping timelines, not top-down tasking.
- Related Felicis piece on talent-market fit (hire remotely, attract people who work well autonomously): [https://www.felicis.com/blog/supabase-talent-market-fit](https://www.felicis.com/blog/supabase-talent-market-fit).
- First Round Review interview on being fully distributed with no offices: [https://review.firstround.com/how-supabase-became-the-essential-infrastructure-for-the-ai-era-paul-copplestone-co-founder-ceo/](https://review.firstround.com/how-supabase-became-the-essential-infrastructure-for-the-ai-era-paul-copplestone-co-founder-ceo/).
- He co-authored the 7 January 2026 security retro, which treats RLS complexity for "developers new to the pattern" as a platform problem to solve with safer defaults and better tooling: [https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-security-2025-retro](https://supabase.com/blog/supabase-security-2025-retro).
